DESCRIPTION

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of flat pasta sheets, rich tomato sauce, creamy béchamel sauce, and melted cheese. Often filled with seasoned meat and herbs, it is baked until golden, bubbly, and delicious. It's a hearty, comforting meal loved for its rich flavours and cheesy goodness.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna pasta sheets
  • Tomato sauce
  • Ground meat (optional)
  • Onion and garlic
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Milk and butter
  • Olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Oregano and basil

Steps

  1. Boil lasagna sheets until soft.
  2. Prepare tomato sauce with onion, garlic and spices.
  3. Cook the meat (optional) in the sauce.
  4. Prepare béchamel sauce using butter, flour and milk.
  5. Layer pasta sheets, tomato sauce, béchamel and cheese.
  6. Repeat the layers and top with mozzarella cheese.
  7. Bake at 180°C for 30-40 minutes until golden.
  8. Let it cool slightly, slice and serve!
