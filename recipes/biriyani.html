DESCRIPTION

Biryani is a popular South Asian rice dish made with fragrant basmati rice, aromatic spices, and marinated meat or vegetables. Cooked with herbs, saffron, and rich flavours, it is known for its delicious aroma and mouthwatering taste.

Ingredients

  • Basmati rice
  • Chicken or vegetables
  • Onions
  • Tomatoes
  • Yogurt
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Biryani masala
  • Whole spices
  • Mint and coriander leaves
  • Saffron
  • Ghee or oil
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Wash and soak basmati rice for 30 minutes.
  2. Marinate chicken with yogurt and biryani masala.
  3. Fry onions until golden brown.
  4. Cook the marinated chicken with spices.
  5. Boil rice with whole spices until partially cooked.
  6. Layer rice and chicken in a pot.
  7. Add mint, coriander and saffron.
  8. Cook on low heat until fully done.
  9. Serve hot with raita.
