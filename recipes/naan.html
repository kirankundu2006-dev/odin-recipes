DESCRIPTION

Naan is a soft, fluffy Indian flatbread made from all-purpose flour, yogurt, and yeast. Traditionally cooked in a tandoor, it has a slightly crispy exterior and a buttery texture. It is commonly served with curries and gravies.

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour (Maida)
  • Yogurt
  • Milk
  • Yeast
  • Sugar
  • Salt
  • Butter or ghee
  • Water

Steps

  1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar and salt.
  2. Add yogurt, milk and water to make a soft dough.
  3. Knead the dough and let it rest for 1-2 hours.
  4. Divide the dough into small balls.
  5. Roll each ball into an oval shape.
  6. Cook on a hot pan or in a tandoor until golden.
  7. Brush with butter or ghee.
  8. Serve hot.
